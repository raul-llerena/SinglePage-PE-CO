Peru

leasein.pe
Main path: quote builder → 4-field modal (name, email, phone, RUC). Alternatives: floating WhatsApp and email.
WHAT WORKS
· Instant pricing visible (from S/179 per month per laptop)
· Strong social proof: 4,000 companies, Forbes, B Corp, testimonials by department
· Clear operational promises: 48h delivery, replacement guarantee, 80+ specialists
WHAT HOLDS IT BACK
· Five products (rental, operating and financial leasing, leaseback, sale) compete before quoting
· Does not explain what happens after submitting the quote
· Form fields lack labels, using placeholders only
· "Services" link leads nowhere and email opens web Gmail

Colombia

leasein.com.co
Main path: on-page form (name, NIT, email, phone, free text). Alternatives: floating WhatsApp and email.
WHAT WORKS
· Form visible right from the first fold
· 4-step process and a promise of contact within 5 minutes
· More focused messaging: computer rental for businesses
WHAT HOLDS IT BACK
· No quote builder or pricing: asks for NIT before the user knows if it fits their budget
· Peruvian client logos displayed (PUCP, María Almenara, Ripley)
· Asks for "DNI" and "vigencia de poder" (Peruvian-specific terms)
· Mixes "renting" and "rental"; two different WhatsApp numbers
CROSS-ANALYSIS

Each site holds half of the solution. Peru convinces with pricing and social proof but scatters visitors across too many products without explaining next steps; Colombia captures leads fast but lacks pricing and local social proof. A unified template leveraging the best of both will deliver the highest return.
