REQUIRED AUDIT STRUCTURE (DELIVERABLES) Please structure the comprehensive audit across the following six strategic phases: #### PHASE 1: Executive Summary & Hypothesis-Driven Diagnosis - Minto Pyramid Principle: Lead with the core conclusion and primary strategic thesis. - Executive Synthesis: Identify the top 3 structural friction points or bottlenecks that typically impede multi-country conversion performance in this sector. - Core Hypothesis Statement: Detail expected performance variances and conversion disparities between Peru and Colombia. #### PHASE 2: Inbound Funnel Architecture & Content-Offer Fit (MECE) Evaluate the lead acquisition funnel through an offer-demand alignment lens across both markets: - TOFU (Attraction & Intent Alignment): Search intent alignment, semantic architecture, and messaging resonance per target market. - MOFU (Nurturing & Value Exchange): Lead magnet value proposition audit. Does the value exchange offer enough leverage to overcome data privacy friction in Peru vs. Colombia? - BOFU (Conversion Friction & Sales Hand-off): Streamlining the pathway from form submission to SDR/Sales Team routing and qualification. #### PHASE 3: CRO, UX/UI & Micro-Conversion Friction Audit - Friction Mapping: Audit cognitive load, form length, field selection, micro-copy, primary/secondary Call-to-Actions (CTAs), and local Trust Signals. - Behavioral Analytics Framework: Define a measurement protocol using Heatmaps, Clickmaps, and Session Recordings (Hotjar/Clarity) to identify user drop-off patterns. - Testing Roadmap: Structure a prioritized A/B and multivariate experimentation roadmap using the ICE (Impact, Confidence, Ease) or RICE framework. #### PHASE 4: Geolocation, International SEO & Local Market Adaptation - Technical SEO Architecture: URL structure strategy (ccTLDs vs. Subdomains vs. Subdirectories), hreflang tag validation, IP routing/canonicalization, and CDN/Core Web Vitals performance per region. - Cultural & Commercial Localization Playbook: Local terminology adaptation, localized social proof (relevant case studies, client logos), currency/pricing structure, and regulatory compliance (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, local data privacy mandates). #### PHASE 5: Analytics Architecture, Unit Economics & KPI Scorecard Align measurement directly with financial unit economics via a comparative KPI scorecard (Peru vs. Colombia): 1. Top-of-Funnel Efficiency: Traffic Volume, Traffic Mix (% Paid vs. Organic), Bounce Rate / Engagement Rate. 2. Conversion Efficiency: Landing Page CVR (Visitor-to-Lead), Form Completion Rate, Micro-conversion Rates. 3. Quality & Velocity Metrics: - MQL-to-SQL Conversion Rate. - Lead Velocity Rate (LVR). - Cost Per Lead (CPL) vs. Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL). 4. RECOMENDACIÓN ESTRATÉGICA: PLATAFORMA UNIFICADA MULTIPAÍS

OPORTUNIDAD DE ALTO ROI

Idea Principal: Unificar leasein.pe y leasein.com.co en una sola plataforma multirregión elimina costos técnicos duplicados, maximiza la autoridad SEO del dominio y reduce drásticamente el CPL. Mantener webs separadas duplica gastos tecnológicos y fragmenta el posicionamiento de marca sin beneficio comercial.

1. HÍBRIDO DE CONVERSIÓN
Combina el configurador de precios al instante y la prueba social de Perú con la captura rápida de leads en primer pantalla y proceso en 4 pasos de Colombia en un único sistema de diseño.
2. AUTORIDAD SEO Y DE MARCA
Consolida el perfil de enlaces y autoridad en un solo dominio raíz, usando etiquetas hreflang para mostrar contenido, moneda y términos legales localizados automáticamente.
3. EFICIENCIA EN DATOS Y MARTECH
Unifica la medición de eventos en GTM, etapas de pipeline en CRM y protocolos de prueba A/B, entregando una fuente única de verdad para la atribución de MQL/SQL por región.
Impacto Principal / Ahorro de Tiempo y Costos:
Reducción del ~35% en Costos de MarTech y Hosting Despliegue ~50% más rápido en Desarrollo y Funcionalidades Reducción del 15-25% en CPL mediante CRO Unificado
