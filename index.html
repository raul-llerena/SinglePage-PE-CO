View Advanced Framework Prompt (Senior McKinsey Consultant Style)
Act as a Senior Engagement Manager from McKinsey & Company specializing in Digital Growth, Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy, and Customer Acquisition for international markets. Your objective is to structure a Master Diagnostic and Digital Optimization Plan (Diagnostic & Growth Playbook) for a company seeking to maximize high-quality lead generation (MQLs and SQLs) through a single website operating simultaneously in two distinct geographic markets: Peru and Colombia. Apply a rigorous, quantitative framework structured around MECE principles (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) and tied directly to core business outcomes (ROI and Unit Economics). --- ### I. OPERATIONAL CONTEXT & CLIENT PARAMETERS - Website URL / Domain Architecture: leasein.pe vs. leasein.com.co - Target Markets: Peru vs. Colombia - Business Model: B2B Laptop Rental & Operational Leasing - Core Value Proposition: Computer hardware leasing and tech infrastructure management for businesses - Primary Lead Magnets / Conversion Points: Quote Request / Contact Form / WhatsApp Lead - Target Buyer Persona per Country: IT Directors, Finance Leads, and Operations Managers in SMEs and Enterprise companies --- ### II. REQUIRED AUDIT STRUCTURE (DELIVERABLES) Please structure the comprehensive audit across the following six strategic phases: #### PHASE 1: Executive Summary & Hypothesis-Driven Diagnosis - Minto Pyramid Principle: Lead with the core conclusion and primary strategic thesis. - Executive Synthesis: Identify the top 3 structural friction points or bottlenecks that typically impede multi-country conversion performance in this sector. - Core Hypothesis Statement: Detail expected performance variances and conversion disparities between Peru and Colombia. #### PHASE 2: Inbound Funnel Architecture & Content-Offer Fit (MECE) Evaluate the lead acquisition funnel through an offer-demand alignment lens across both markets: - TOFU (Attraction & Intent Alignment): Search intent alignment, semantic architecture, and messaging resonance per target market. - MOFU (Nurturing & Value Exchange): Lead magnet value proposition audit. Does the value exchange offer enough leverage to overcome data privacy friction in Peru vs. Colombia? - BOFU (Conversion Friction & Sales Hand-off): Streamlining the pathway from form submission to SDR/Sales Team routing and qualification. #### PHASE 3: CRO, UX/UI & Micro-Conversion Friction Audit - Friction Mapping: Audit cognitive load, form length, field selection, micro-copy, primary/secondary Call-to-Actions (CTAs), and local Trust Signals. - Behavioral Analytics Framework: Define a measurement protocol using Heatmaps, Clickmaps, and Session Recordings (Hotjar/Clarity) to identify user drop-off patterns. - Testing Roadmap: Structure a prioritized A/B and multivariate experimentation roadmap using the ICE (Impact, Confidence, Ease) or RICE framework. #### PHASE 4: Geolocation, International SEO & Local Market Adaptation - Technical SEO Architecture: URL structure strategy (ccTLDs vs. Subdomains vs. Subdirectories), hreflang tag validation, IP routing/canonicalization, and CDN/Core Web Vitals performance per region. - Cultural & Commercial Localization Playbook: Local terminology adaptation, localized social proof (relevant case studies, client logos), currency/pricing structure, and regulatory compliance (e.g., GDPR, CCPA, local data privacy mandates). #### PHASE 5: Analytics Architecture, Unit Economics & KPI Scorecard Align measurement directly with financial unit economics via a comparative KPI scorecard (Peru vs. Colombia): 1. Top-of-Funnel Efficiency: Traffic Volume, Traffic Mix (% Paid vs. Organic), Bounce Rate / Engagement Rate. 2. Conversion Efficiency: Landing Page CVR (Visitor-to-Lead), Form Completion Rate, Micro-conversion Rates. 3. Quality & Velocity Metrics: - MQL-to-SQL Conversion Rate. - Lead Velocity Rate (LVR). - Cost Per Lead (CPL) vs. Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL). 4. Unit Economics Bridge: Impact analysis on Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and LTV:CAC ratio by geographic market. #### PHASE 6: Prioritized Strategic Roadmap & Implementation Plan Map actionable recommendations onto an Impact vs. Effort (2x2) Matrix: - Wave 1: Quick Wins / Low-Hanging Fruit (Weeks 1–4) — High-impact UX, CTA, micro-copy, and form optimizations. - Wave 2: Core Optimization & MarTech Integration (Weeks 5–12) — Funnel redesign, technical SEO remediation, lead scoring, and CRM routing automation. - Wave 3: Strategic Transformation & Personalization (Months 3+) — Dynamic IP/geolocation personalization, advanced cohort-based landing page optimization. --- ### III. FORMAT & TONALITY - Tone: Executive, analytical, incisive, data-backed, and highly professional. - Structure: Utilize Markdown tables for the KPI Scorecard and the Prioritization Matrix. - Before delivering the final report, indicate if you require any specific clarification regarding the business model, current metrics, or target markets, or proceed directly to generating the full strategic proposal.

RECOMENDACIÓN ESTRATÉGICA: PLATAFORMA UNIFICADA MULTIPAÍS

ALTO ROI

Idea Principal: Unificar leasein.pe y leasein.com.co en una sola plataforma multirregión elimina costos técnicos duplicados, maximiza la autoridad SEO del dominio y reduce drastically el CPL. Mantener webs separadas duplica gastos tecnológicos y fragmenta marca sin beneficio.

1. HÍBRIDO CONVERSIÓN
Combina el cotizador inmediato de Perú con la captura veloz en primer pantalla de Colombia.
2. AUTORIDAD SEO
Consolida backlinks bajo un solo dominio usando hreflang por país.
3. MARTECH & DATOS
Unifica la atribución de GTM y CRM bajo un único flujo MQL/SQL regional.
Ahorro de Tiempo y Costos:
~35% Menos Hosting/MarTech ~50% Devs más rápidos 15-25% Reducción CPL
